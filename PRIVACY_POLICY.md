# SiriMetra Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 2026**

## Overview

SiriMetra ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other privacy laws.

**In short: We collect nothing. Everything stays on your device.**

## 1. Data We Store

The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

| Data | Purpose | Storage |
|------|---------|---------|
| Home station preference | Answer "next train home" queries | Device only (UserDefaults) |
| Work station preference | Answer "next train to work" queries | Device only (UserDefaults) |
| Notification settings | Control delay/alert notifications | Device only (UserDefaults) |
| Preferred Metra lines | Personalize schedule display | Device only (UserDefaults) |
| GDPR consent record | Legal compliance (Art. 7) | Device only (UserDefaults) |

## 2. Data We Do NOT Collect

- Location data
- Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV)
- IP addresses
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports
- Personally identifiable information
- Biometric data
- Financial data
- Health data
- Contact information

## 3. Network Requests

The App makes requests **only** to Metra's public GTFS API (`gtfsapi.metrarail.com`) to fetch:
- Train schedules
- Real-time delay information
- Service alerts

**No user data is included in these requests.** The requests contain no cookies, no authentication tokens, and no identifying information.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **NOT** share your data with:
- Advertisers
- Analytics providers
- Data brokers
- Any third parties
- Our own servers (we don't have any)

## 5. Third-Party SDKs

The App uses **zero** third-party SDKs. No analytics frameworks, no ad networks, no crash reporting tools, no social media SDKs.

## 6. Data Security

All data is stored in iOS UserDefaults, protected by:
- Device passcode/biometric authentication
- iOS data protection (encrypted at rest)
- App sandboxing

We operate no servers, databases, or cloud infrastructure.

## 7. Your Rights Under GDPR

You can exercise all of the following rights directly from the **Privacy Dashboard** in the app's Settings:

### Article 15 — Right of Access
View all data stored about you in the Privacy Dashboard.

### Article 17 — Right to Erasure
Delete all your data instantly with one tap. This is irreversible and removes all stored preferences.

### Article 20 — Right to Data Portability
Export all your data as a machine-readable JSON file that you can save or share.

### Article 7 — Right to Withdraw Consent
Withdraw your consent at any time. This will delete all stored data and reset the app to its initial state.

### Article 16 — Right to Rectification
Modify any of your preferences at any time through the app's Settings screen.

## 8. Data Retention

Data is retained on your device until:
- You explicitly delete it via the Privacy Dashboard
- You uninstall the App
- You withdraw consent

There is no automatic data expiration because no data exists outside your device.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from children or anyone else. Since all data is stored locally and we collect no personal information, the App is suitable for users of all ages.

## 10. International Data Transfers

There are no international data transfers because no data leaves your device. The only network traffic is to Metra's public API servers for schedule data.

## 11. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

The legal basis for storing preferences on your device is **consent** (Article 6(1)(a)). You are asked for explicit consent before any data is stored, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

## 12. Data Protection Officer

Given that we collect no personal data and operate no servers, we have not appointed a Data Protection Officer. For privacy inquiries, please contact us through the App Store listing.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be communicated through an app update. You will be shown the updated policy and asked to re-consent before the app stores any data.

## 14. Supervisory Authority

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.
